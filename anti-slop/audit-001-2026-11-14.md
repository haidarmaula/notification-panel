## Assessment

The forest/off-white identity, readable titles, explicit statuses, and clear
primary campaign action are useful foundations. The main issue is a mismatch
between available content and its allocated visual space. Spoctor also contains
large empty areas, but richer record grouping, distinct panel regions and
typographic contrast make that space feel intentional.

The following are design findings, not claims of broken functionality. Severity
P2 means a worthwhile usability/design refinement. Anti-slop priority follows
the cited rule tier.

## Numbered findings

1. **LOW / P2: Oversized rows and column allocation for sparse content.**
   Rule R-05: composition should follow actual content needs. `styles.css:968`
   gives cells 18px vertical padding; `:976` allocates 60% to the campaign name.
   `List.tsx:111` renders only title, ID, status and an arrow. Captures show roughly
   80px rows with a long visual gap before status. Trial 12-14px vertical padding,
   tighter column sizing, and retained 40px action targets; verify long titles and
   mobile behavior. Do not solve density by shrinking every label.

2. **MEDIUM / P2: Row hierarchy gives limited recognition cues.**
   Rule R-06: typography must serve identity and hierarchy. Overview and template
   names use ordinary links while the campaign list has a dedicated semibold
   title. IDs occupy the only secondary text line. Spoctor distinguishes name,
   type, and technical metadata with compact spacing and different text styles.
   Standardize a record title/metadata treatment and align status/action anchors.
   Add icons only when they communicate record type or state (R-04), not merely
   to fill space. `CampaignSummary` currently exposes only id/title/status
   (`src/lib/api/generated/staff.ts:598`); dates, recipients and message excerpts
   must not be invented or fetched per row as a cosmetic workaround.

3. **MEDIUM / P2: Navigation cards use content-panel proportions.**
   Rule R-14: cards should serve a content purpose. `Overview.tsx:75` presents
   three title/description links; `.surface` at `styles.css:1199` applies 27px
   padding to both navigation and actual detail content. Captures show similarly
   weighted boxes without a strong action cue. Give navigation tiles their own
   compact proportions, clear destination affordance, and hover/focus treatment.
   For actual content panels, separate heading, body and footer rather than
   applying one undifferentiated inset to everything.

4. **MEDIUM / P2: Weak contrast between content roles produces flatness.**
   Rules R-06 and R-07: typography and backgrounds must communicate purpose.
   `.section-bar h2` and `.surface h2` are 15px, near 14px body text. In the dark
   capture, canvas, cards and table surfaces form similar green fields. Spoctor
   uses larger panel headings, technical metadata and distinct charcoal layers.
   Preserve Hello's forest identity while strengthening title/body/metadata
   hierarchy and differentiating canvas, panel and header surfaces. Validate
   contrast in both themes; color similarity alone is not a WCAG failure.

5. **LOW / P2: Sparse-data page composition leaves an unstructured void.**
   Rule R-05: whitespace should organize content. `.campaign-data-region` at
   `styles.css:495` reserves a minimum 360px; `.page-heading` also has 37px bottom
   margin. The one-row capture shows substantial blank space before the footer.
   Some reserved space deliberately stabilizes loading/empty transitions; retain
   that behavior while testing a smaller or adaptive reservation and a cohesive
   toolbar/table/pagination panel. Do not fill the page with fabricated metrics,
   charts, or unrelated cards. Spoctor's graph needs open space for spatial
   relationships; a short tabular list has a different content requirement.

## Proposed direction, pending selection

Compact operational workspace with clear record hierarchy and articulated panel
structure. Preserve forest green, existing behavior, and light/dark support.
Suggested dials: ENERGY 1, RHYTHM 2, MOTION 1. Start with findings 1, 3, 4 and 5;
apply finding 2 within the existing API contract. Typeface replacement is not a
prerequisite. No imitation of Spoctor's fraud graph or orange palette is needed.
